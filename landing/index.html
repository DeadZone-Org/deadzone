01 the dead zone

Every wallet needs the
internet — until the one
moment you don't have it.

Crypto promised money without banks. But every wallet still quietly depends on a server. The second you lose signal — a basement, a blackout, a packed stadium, a network shutdown — your money stops working. Deadzone removes that last dependency.

01 — sign

Offline

An EIP-3009 payment signed on your phone. No internet, no gas, no popup.

02 — relay

Bluetooth

Fragments hop phone-to-phone over BLE until one finds signal.

03 — decide

The agent

The gateway agent validates, rejects bad payments, batches the rest.

04 — settle

Mantle

It pre-commits the decision to ERC-8004, then settles on-chain.

05 — earn

Reputation

The courier attests the outcome & earns verifiable on-chain trust.

for honest delivery.
